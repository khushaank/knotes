Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

1. Use of Service

By using K. Notes, you agree to follow our Guidelines and interact respectfully with other users.

2. Content Ownership

You retain ownership of the content you submit. However, by posting, you grant K. Notes a non-exclusive license to display and distribute that content.

3. Information We Process

We process email, username, authentication session data, members-only posts, comments, votes, uploaded images, feedback, and basic usage and security logs. Community contributions and profiles are available only to approved members using verified two-factor authentication. They are not public.

4. Service Providers and Storage

Supabase provides authentication, database, and private file storage. Cloudflare and GitHub Pages deliver the site and may process connection metadata such as IP address and user agent. Google Fonts may receive font requests, and opening an optional YouTube embed connects your browser to YouTube. Application libraries are served from K. Notes itself. The browser stores authentication and preference data, but not persistent copies of private posts. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and Your Choices

Account and community content is retained while your account is active. Security logs are retained for up to 90 days unless a longer period is required to investigate abuse or comply with law. Deleted account data is removed from the active database promptly; encrypted backups expire on the hosting provider's backup schedule. Request access, correction, export, or deletion at uniquevara.ai@gmail.com. We will acknowledge requests and provide status by email.

6. Acceptable Use and Moderation

Do not upload malware, violate intellectual-property rights, attempt unauthorized access, automate abuse, or use the service to harm others. We may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these terms or the community guidelines.

7. Children and Changes

K. Notes is not directed to children under 13. Material changes to these terms will be identified here. Last updated: July 23, 2026.

8. Security and Contact

K. Notes is access-controlled, not end-to-end encrypted. Supabase project administrators and authorized infrastructure operators can technically access stored content. No online service can guarantee absolute security. A strong password and verified authenticator factor are required; see our Security Policy for vulnerability reporting.
